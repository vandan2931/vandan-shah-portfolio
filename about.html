Who I Am

I'm Vandan Shah, a Shopify Developer with hands-on experience developing, customizing, and optimizing production e-commerce storefronts using Shopify Liquid, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. I've built custom Shopify sections, product and collection pages, cart drawers, responsive layouts, theme customizations, and third-party app integrations across multiple stores.

With a strong frontend background in React.js, Node.js, and REST APIs, I translate design requirements into scalable, production-ready solutions. I've worked on brands including WheyMill, Bolt Nutritions, Evis Healthcare, Kttell, and Avironn.
